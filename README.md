#This is a python-data analysis system, made by the team to process a dataset from kraggle about the impact of AI n the job market and jobs in general. It allows users to analyse salary trends automation risk, growth factors, and skills

#Ai impact on jobs 2030
#Available on kaggle at https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/khushikyad001/ai-impact-on-jobs-2030

#Dataset contains approximately 3000 rows and exactly 18 columns
